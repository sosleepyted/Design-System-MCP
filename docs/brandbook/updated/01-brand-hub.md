# Welcome to the UCM Brand Hub

This is your go-to space for staying on-brand across all visuals, content, and
touchpoints.

## Why it matters

Our brand is more than a logo. It is the feeling we create through every
interaction. Consistent, clear, and thoughtful design builds trust and
recognition.

## The UCM Brand Guide

Explore the full Brand Guide for detailed principles on voice, design,
personas, and more.

The guide is both toolkit and rulebook. The core rules below are
non-negotiable and enforced in our tooling, so work that follows them comes
out on-brand by default. Everything else is guidance to help you be a
confident UCM brand ambassador.

## Core principles

- Be real: use natural, helpful, and confident language. Brand surfaces may be
  motivational; product surfaces stay informational, never opinionated.
- Stay consistent: stick to the defined colors, typography, and layout
  structure. Precision minimalism means exact values, not vibes.
- Design with clarity: hierarchy comes from contrast, not decoration. Visuals
  support the message, they never compete with it.

## Two modes, never mixed

- Brand mode is the external website: yellow CTAs, glow, expressive radii,
  motivational voice.
- Product mode is the internal app: navy actions, restrained, no glow,
  informational voice.
- Shared components work in both modes. Never mix the two on one surface.

## Essentials at a glance

### Logo

Only use the files from the official branding elements folder:
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1I6p89HNb2kteBE6LIKWr4fAlxixvjdFb
(Google Drive, UCM account required). The wordmark is set in Nunito
Extra-Bold 800; Nunito is never used for anything else. Follow spacing,
sizing, and contrast rules from the Brand Guide.

### Typography

Our typeface is Figtree, loaded as a variable font (weights 300 to 900) with
the latin-ext subset so it covers ä ö ü ß.

Weights in practice:

- Light 300 with tight tracking for headings and display text. Light headings
  are the brand voice on every surface.
- Regular 400 for body text.
- Medium 500 for section headings, Semi-Bold 600 for subheadings.
- Nunito Extra-Bold 800 for the wordmark only.

### Color palette

The whole system is one triad: cream page, navy ink, yellow accent. Do not
introduce new hues.

| Name | HEX | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| Page (cream) | #F5F5F3 | Page background |
| Ink (navy) | #001E2B | Text and borders, used everywhere instead of black |
| Accent (yellow) | #FCC224 | The accent and the brand primary CTA; hover #FFD84D |
| Success | #0F5E2A | Success text and tint base |
| Danger | #A32D2D | Danger text and button background |
| Card | #FFFFFF | Card surface, the one allowed pure white |
| Cream tint | #F8F5EE | Pill chips, soft callouts |

Rules that keep the palette honest:

- Never pure black. #001E2B is the ink everywhere.
- Pure white is a card surface only, never the page and never text.
- Yellow stays scarce: never more than about 10 percent of a surface.
- Borders are always navy with alpha (#001E2B at 8 to 25 percent), never gray
  and never black.
- Warning states use the accent family: #8A5A00 as text on a yellow tint,
  #A96B00 as inline warning text.
- A small decorative sub-palette exists (teals such as #03677E, light blue
  #C2E8FF, brand orange #F0A639). Decorative only, never the CTA.

### Iconography and writing

- No emoji anywhere: not in UI, not in documents, not in headings. All
  iconography is the Material Symbols Icon set; where no icon fits, use a
  plain word.
- No em dashes in any copy. Use commas, colons, or parentheses. En dashes are
  for numeric ranges only.
- No eyebrows (uppercase letter-spaced kickers above headings). Uppercase is
  reserved for acronyms like UCM and for data.
- Every user-visible product string exists in German and English, German
  first.
