`Card` groups related content on a single white surface with the documented
product shadow and the navy alpha border. It is deliberately plain so the content
leads.

## When to use

**Use a card:**
- to group a unit of related content or a single record
- to lift a primary action and its context off the page

**Don't use a card:**
- inside another card (use a `Divider` to separate content instead)
- as a decorative box around everything on the page

Compose the inside with `Stack`, a heading, body copy, and one action. Keep the
emphasis on the content, not the container.

## Behaviour

### Placement

Cards sit in a `Grid` or a `Stack`. In product mode they never nest; a second
level of grouping uses a `Divider`, not a card inside a card.

## Accessibility

- A card is a plain surface; give it a `role` and `aria-label` only when it is a
  meaningful group.
- Keep the contrast of content on the white surface above the minimum.
- Do not convey state with the surface alone; pair it with text or an icon.

## Content

- Lead with a short heading, then one or two lines of body copy.
- Keep to a single primary action per card.
- Bilingual (DE and EN), sentence case, no em dashes.
