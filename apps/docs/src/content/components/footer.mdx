`Footer` closes the page: a wordmark with a short tagline, columns of links, and a
bottom bar for the legal line and small actions. One component serves both
surfaces through the `mode` prop.

## When to use

**Use Footer:**
- as the closing navigation on any brand or product page
- to group secondary links (product, company, legal)

**Don't use Footer:**
- for the primary navigation (use `Nav`)
- as a dumping ground for every link on the site

## Behaviour

### Placement

`Footer` sits at the bottom of the page after the main content. The link columns
stack to two on small screens and the bottom bar wraps below the links.

## Accessibility

- Renders a `<footer>` `contentinfo` landmark, with a labelled `<nav>` for the
  link columns.
- Column titles are plain sentence-case labels, never eyebrows.
- Links meet the 36px minimum touch target.
- On the dark brand surface, link color contrast is kept above the minimum and
  hover does not rely on color alone (the underline and lift cues remain).

## Content

- Column titles are one noun, bilingual (DE and EN).
- Link labels are short and bilingual. No em dashes, sentence case.
- The legal line carries the year and entity, nothing more.
