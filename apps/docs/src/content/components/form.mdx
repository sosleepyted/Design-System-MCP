The form set covers the common product inputs. `Field` owns the label and the id
and aria wiring; `Input`, `Select` and `Checkbox` are the controls. Empty or
attention states use the documented yellow ring, the one place the accent appears
in a form.

## When to use

**Use the form set:**
- for any input the user fills in the internal app
- when a control needs a label, a hint, or an error tied to it

**Don't:**
- place an input without a `Field` label (a bare placeholder is not a label)
- use the yellow ring as decoration; it signals an empty or attention state

`Field` renders the label and, below the control, either a hint or, when set, an
error. The control reads its id, `aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid`, and
`aria-required` from the field, so wiring is automatic.

## Behaviour

### States

- Default: navy alpha border, darkening on hover and focus.
- Attention: the yellow border and its ring partner, for empty or attention
  fields.
- Error: `Field` shows the error text and marks the control invalid.

## Accessibility

- `Field` connects the `<label>` to the control with a shared id, and ties hint
  and error text via `aria-describedby`.
- An error sets `aria-invalid`; a required field sets `aria-required`.
- Controls are at least 36px tall; the checkbox row is the full label.
- Focus is always visible on the navy focus ring.

## Content

- Labels are one or two words, bilingual (DE and EN), sentence case.
- Errors say what to do, not just what went wrong, and carry no em dashes.
